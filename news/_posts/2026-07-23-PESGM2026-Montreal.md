* We contributed three conference papers and chaired/co-chaired two panels including IEEE PES Outstanding Dissertation Panel and a technical panel described below.

* We also proposed a new IEEE PES Task Force, and Working Group, with colleagues, to help advance collaboration and technical development in frequency-secure grid operations, and enhance grid energy management and decision-making processes.

#### Technical Panel Organization and Moderation:

Xingpeng Li and Jonghwan Kwon of Argonne National Laboratory organized and moderated the panel:

**Panel: Frequency-Constrained Grid Operations for Low-Inertia Power Systems**

The panel brought together perspectives from a national laboratory, a grid operator, academia, industry research, and a technology company. The speakers were:

- Jin Tan, National Laboratory of the Rockies - formerly known as NREL.
- Yunzhi Cheng / Amro Quedan, Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT).
- Yichen Zhang, The University of Texas at Arlington.
- Deepak Ramasubramanian, Electric Power Research Institute (EPRI).
- Duncan Burt, Reactive Technologies.

I delivered the opening remarks, highlighting the growing need to connect inertia and frequency monitoring, frequency-aware energy management, grid-forming resources, and practical scheduling and operational decision support.
