"From May to August 2026, <a href="/people/Haoxiang-Wan/" class="" target="_blank">Haoxiang Wan</a> worked as a summer research intern in the Energy Systems and Infrastructure Assessment Division at Argonne National Laboratory, supervised by Dr. Jonghwan Kwon. He then used it to study generation expansion planning under uncertain data center deployment in Texas, which led to a first-authored journal paper with Argonne researchers. Through the internship, Haoxiang gained hands-on experience with national laboratory planning tools and high-performance computing."
