* I attended the 2026 IEEE IAS Annual Meeting at the Vancouver Convention Centre, Vancouver, Canada, October 4–8, 2026.

* I chaired four technical paper sessions and presented three of our papers.

* It was also good to catch up with my former Ph.D. student <a target="_blank" href="/people/Cunzhi-Zhao/" class="">Cunzhi Zhao</a>, now a professor at McNeese State University.


<div class="bigspacer"></div>


#### RPGLab Conference Papers:

Our papers addressed three areas in power system planning and operation:

**1. <a class="" target="_blank" href="/papers/Haoxiang-DC-Sflex-SCUC/">Data Center Spatio-Temporal Load Flexibility in Security-Constrained Unit Commitment for Enhanced Grid Efficiency and Reliability</a>**

* Haoxiang Wan, and Xingpeng Li.
* Data center load flexibility: coordinating workloads across locations and time within security-constrained unit commitment (SCUC) to reduce transmission congestion and renewable curtailment.

![](/images/news/2026.10.08_IASAM/p01.jpeg)
<p></p>
<span class="text-figure-legend"  style="font-size:15px;">Renewable curtailment with fixed vs. flexible data center loads.</span>

<div class="spacer"></div>

**2. <a class="" target="_blank" href="/papers/Linhan-DisGrd_DC-Hosting-Cap/">Hosting Capacity Assessment and Enhancement for Edge Data Centers in Active Distribution Networks</a>**

* Linhan Fang, and Xingpeng Li.
* Edge data center hosting capacity: identifying distribution network limits and evaluating how battery storage, local generation, and reactive power support can accommodate more data center load.

![](/images/news/2026.10.08_IASAM/p02.png)
<p></p>
<span class="text-figure-legend"  style="font-size:15px;">Maximum data center hosting capacity at each bus of the distribution network.</span>

<div class="spacer"></div>

**3. <a class="" target="_blank" href="/papers/Fan-SCUC-BESS-GFM/">Learning-Assisted Day-Ahead Energy Scheduling for Frequency-Secure Inverter-Dominated Grids with Grid-Forming Battery Energy Storage Systems</a>**

* Fan Jiang, and Xingpeng Li.
* Learning-assisted grid operation: using models trained on EMT simulations to incorporate grid-forming battery dynamics and reserve requirements into frequency-secure day-ahead energy scheduling.

![](/images/news/2026.10.08_IASAM/p03.png)
<p></p>
<span class="text-figure-legend"  style="font-size:15px;">Hourly RoCoF and frequency under three day-ahead scheduling models.</span>
