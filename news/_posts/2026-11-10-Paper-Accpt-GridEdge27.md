Congratulations to <a href="/people/Haoxiang-Wan/" class="off" target="_blank">Haoxiang Wan</a>, <a href="/people/Linhan-Fang/" class="off" target="_blank">Linhan Fang</a>, and <a href="/people/Rida-Fatima/" class="off" target="_blank">Rida Fatima</a> for their papers accepted by the 2027 IEEE PES Grid Edge Technologies Conference &amp; Exposition.

Conference website: https://pes-gridedge.org/

Conference Date: April 19 - April 22, 2027.

Conference Location: Salt Lake City, Utah, USA.

##### Our papers accepted at IEEE PES Grid Edge 2027 are as follows:

* <a href="/papers/Haoxiang-SecFid-DC-HC/" class="" target="_blank">Haoxiang Wan, Linhan Fang, Jonghwan Kwon, Jesus Silva-Rodriguez, Jianyi Yang, Jaya Yellajosula, Fan Jiang, and Xingpeng Li, "Impact of Security Modeling Fidelity on Hyperscale Data Center Hosting Capacity Assessment", <em>IEEE PES Grid Edge Technologies Conference &amp; Exposition</em>, Salt Lake City, UT, USA, Apr. 2027.</a>

* <a href="/papers/Linhan-SA-EdgeDC-HC/" class="" target="_blank">Linhan Fang, Haoxiang Wan, Anurag Srivastava, and Xingpeng Li, "Service-Aware Hosting Capacity Assessment of Edge Data Centers with Workload Flexibility", <em>IEEE PES Grid Edge Technologies Conference &amp; Exposition</em>, Salt Lake City, UT, USA, Apr. 2027.</a>

* <a href="/papers/Rida-EV-DER-DistRecgrtn/" class="" target="_blank">Rida Fatima, Linhan Fang, and Xingpeng Li, "Active Power Thermal Feasibility Assessment of EV Integration with DER Scheduling and Distribution Network Reconfiguration", <em>IEEE PES Grid Edge Technologies Conference &amp; Exposition</em>, Salt Lake City, UT, USA, Apr. 2027.</a>
